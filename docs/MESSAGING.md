# XTRA-CASH messaging: Put out the fire

Money trouble feels like a fire: it starts small, spreads fast and panic makes it worse. XTRA-CASH is the extinguisher you already carry. Every campaign, screen and conversation runs on one line: **Put out the fire.**

The marketing team's working copy is a shared doc (https://claude.ai/code/artifact/3cf15a04-b7c2-4282-b952-9747d3fc6bb4). This file is the version the product is built from; keep the two in step.

## Platform

| Element | Line |
| --- | --- |
| Master line (headline) | **Put out the fire.** |
| Supporting line | Money trouble spreads fast. XTRA-CASH stops it at the till, in seconds. |
| Sign-off on every piece | XTRA-CASH. Put out the fire. |
| Call to action | Get my card |

- **The fire is always the problem, never the product.** We don't sell credit; we sell the moment the panic stops.
- **The extinguisher is the card.** It pays from your own money first, then covers the shortfall with the cheapest matched lender offer.
- **Visual system:** burning artwork shows the problem, the brand gradient shows the fix, and the frozen artwork shows control. The burning video opens the website and the mobile app's welcome screen.

## The four beats

| Beat | What the customer feels | What we say | Visual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. The spark | Short at the till, school fees due, taxi fare gone, the fridge breaks | "It starts with one bill." | Burning card, burning logo |
| 2. Put it out | Taps the XTRA-CASH card; the shortfall is covered in seconds | "Tap. Covered. Fire out." | Burning video resolving to the brand gradient |
| 3. Cool down | Fixed repayments, full cost shown upfront, settle early with no penalty | "No smoke. No hidden fees." | Half-burning, half-frozen card |
| 4. Fireproof | Affordability checks, building up the wallet, freezing the card any time | "Stay cool. Stay in control." | Frozen card, frozen logo |

Beats 3 and 4 carry the responsible-lending message, so no campaign stops at beat 2.

## Proof points

- **Speed:** approved at the till in seconds.
- **Your money first:** the card pays from your wallet before any credit is used.
- **Full cost upfront:** you see the installment, total cost and fees before you confirm.
- **Fixed payments:** 1 to 24 months, settle early any time with no penalty.
- **Only what you can afford:** every offer passes an affordability check.
- **Registered lenders:** credit comes from independent lenders registered with the National Credit Regulator (NCR).

## Compliance guardrails

XTRA-CASH advertises credit in South Africa, so the National Credit Act and the NCR's rules on credit advertising apply. A compliance officer signs off every campaign before launch.

- The fire is the customer's problem, never a reason to borrow more. No "spend now", "treat yourself" or "free money" angles.
- Any ad that mentions credit terms shows the cost: interest rate, fees and a worked example.
- Never say "no credit check", "guaranteed approval" or "instant cash for everyone".
- Every campaign includes a beat 3 or beat 4 message.
- Show the lender's NCR registration where the product is offered.

## Copy do's and don'ts

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| "Short at the till? Put out the fire." | "Need cash? Borrow now!" |
| "See the full cost before you pay." | "No hidden fees*" (with small print) |
| "Fixed payments you can afford." | "Guaranteed approval, no credit check." |
| "Money trouble spreads fast. We stop it at the till." | "Your life is on fire!" (shaming or scary) |
| "Stay cool. Freeze your card any time." | "Spend more with XTRA-CASH." |
| Plain words: wallet, credit, repayment, cost | Jargon: facility, drawdown, APR without explanation |

Keep the fire imagery about the situation, never about the person. Every piece ends with the sign-off: **XTRA-CASH. Put out the fire.**
