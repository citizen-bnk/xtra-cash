# Restaurant QR checkout launch plan

## Current implementation (14 November 2026)

`apps/web/src/app/scanqr/` contains a responsive checkout for `/scanqr/`. It supports camera or file selection, draggable bill corners, browser-based OCR, review and correction of bill fields and line items. A signed-in customer can create a server-backed table bill and share its opaque link. Every open bill polls the same record every three seconds; item claims are serialized in the database and show the claimant's first name and status. The payer sees their wallet balance, and payment is disabled if it is insufficient. The API has an atomic wallet debit, balanced ledger entries, item paid status, one itemised receipt per payer, a final in-app notification record, and original scan storage. This wallet payment endpoint is **off by default** and additionally requires an active merchant QR record with a configured settlement reference and verified EFT recipient token. The separate sample identity, loan offer, repayment selection, terms, signature and receipt remain a DEMO. OCR output is unverified until the diner confirms it. There is no live top-up collection, automated EFT submission, email delivery, or restaurant BNPL underwriting yet. The receipt library also scans and stores unrelated purchases with the original image and extracted details.

The signed-in Pay screen links to `/scanqr/` and shows wallet plus eligible credit on a card-style balance panel. It offers a zero-wallet Top up funds CTA, but production top-up collection is disabled until a payment provider is connected. Its Get a loan action opens the personal-loan journey; the restaurant checkout remains a separate preview.

The restaurant registry now has a long opaque QR identifier and a printed six character code in the format `ABC123` (three letters followed by three digits). Scanning `<checkout-base>/scanqr/?merchant=<opaque-id>` resolves the restaurant automatically; a QR without that parameter asks for the printed code before bill capture. The default checkout base is `https://xtra-cash.vercel.app`, and `RESTAURANT_CHECKOUT_BASE_URL` can change it after the requested `xtra-cash.co.za` route is verified live. The API rejects a live bill without a valid active restaurant code. The restaurant portal at `/restaurant` shows wallet-paid items, available and requested amounts, QR link, printed code, and payout history. Admin can onboard a restaurant, assign an existing account, configure a verified bank recipient token and masked destination, set daily or on-demand mode, and record a bank-confirmed outcome. The daily job queues eligible paid items at 00:15 South Africa time. Each item can enter one pending payout, so repeated requests cannot reserve it twice. A bill becomes settled only after every paid item is in a bank-confirmed payout. **Automated EFT submission is not yet connected**; queued requests are not payment proof.

## Launch customer journey

1. Scan a restaurant-specific QR code linking to `https://xtra-cash.co.za/scanqr/?merchant=<opaque-id>`. If the identifier is absent, enter the `ABC123` style code printed beneath the acrylic QR. The app verifies the restaurant and shows its trading name before bill capture. Detect browser capabilities, not only user-agent, and show a phone-sized checkout on mobile and a centered phone panel with explanatory side panel on desktop.
2. Take a bill photo or upload one. Detect its corners, let the diner adjust the crop, flatten it, then use OCR to extract restaurant, bill reference, items, tip and total. The diner confirms or edits every amount. Bind the bill to the merchant and table, detect duplicates, and confirm the merchant is active. Allow manual entry when OCR fails.
3. The scanning diner shares the opaque table link. Each diner signs up or logs in, claims their own items, and sees names plus live claim/payment states. The server serializes claims and rejects double claims. The bill link expires after 24 hours. Shared items and tip must be represented as separate split lines before creating the bill. Group editing, refunds and claim timeouts remain to be built.
4. Each diner chooses **Pay my share in full** or **Check a loan for my share**. The loan is individual to that diner. For credit, collect or retrieve their ID through a secure field, explicit credit-check consent and authentication or registration. Reuse the customer profile and vault. Run identity, credit bureau and affordability checks; request income evidence only when needed. Never show a numeric credit offer before a real lender decision.
5. Show the lender's actual pre-agreement statement and quotation for that diner's items, immediate payment, XTRA-CASH funded amount, rates, fees, total repayable and dates. The diner can accept, choose a repayment method and complete any required mandate.
6. Present the full agreement, collect a clear acceptance and electronic signature, and store a tamper-evident document and consent trail. The lending provider countersigns or authorizes per policy.
7. The diner must first have enough money in their XTRA-CASH wallet. Top-up or approved lending must fund the wallet through verified source and ledger events. At checkout, debit the wallet once and credit the approved restaurant payable account in one database transaction; mark only that diner's claimed items paid. No card or bank choice appears at restaurant checkout.
8. Once every item is paid, mark the bill **funded** and notify participants in-app. Paid items increase the restaurant's available payout total. The restaurant can request an on-demand payout; daily mode queues unreserved paid items after the trading day. The EFT integration must submit the payout using its unique reference and confirm the bank outcome before marking it paid. Only then mark fully funded bills **settled**. Issue each diner an itemized platform receipt and retain the original bill. Email receipts only after an email provider and verified delivery status are configured. Handle partial failure with timeout, cancellation or refund and reconciliation.

## Delivery sequence

1. **Merchant pilot and UX** — signed QR payloads, merchant/table registry, responsive public route, clear bill capture, review and pay-in-full path; no live credit claims.
2. **Bill ingestion** — secure upload storage, OCR provider, confidence scores, manual correction, merchant/bill validation, idempotency and duplicate protection.
3. **Credit** — login/registration handoff, profile reuse, consent, lender-funded BNPL product, bureau and affordability integrations, lender authorization, compliant quotation and agreement templates.
4. **Money movement** — approved wallet top-up rail, wallet ledger, lender funding into wallet, merchant payable reconciliation, payout rail, webhooks and refunds. Cards or bank accounts are relevant to wallet top-ups, not the table checkout.
5. **Operations and launch** — merchant reporting, exception queue, support, reconciliation, audit logs, device testing at pilot restaurants, and a controlled rollout.

## Decisions and access needed

- Pilot restaurant legal entity, merchant bank account and signed merchant agreement; each table's QR ownership and bill numbering.
- Licensed/accredited credit provider for this product, approved underwriting and pricing, credit limits, settlement reserve and who bears fraud, chargebacks and defaults.
- Approved restaurant merchant ID, opaque QR code, settlement reference, signed agreement and payout destination. Only an approved QR and `RESTAURANT_WALLET_PAYMENTS_ENABLED=true` enable the wallet debit endpoint.
- A verified wallet funding rail for top-ups and a merchant EFT provider with recipient creation, transfer submission, status webhooks, bank statement reconciliation, credentials and sandbox access. Internal wallet debits do not create funds or transfer them to the restaurant's bank account. Until this connection is built, admin can record a confirmed external EFT reference; requesting a payout alone never marks it paid.
- An email delivery service and verified sending domain for individual receipts. Phone-only accounts need a verified email address before an email can be sent.
- Bank account/mandate provider if debit orders or bank-linked repayments are required. A bank-linking service for data access does not itself authorize future debits.
- OCR, ID/KYC, bureau, income/affordability and electronic-signature provider accounts and sandbox credentials.
- Final agreement, consent, privacy and collections wording reviewed for the specific credit product and payment structure.

**Release gate:** The table can show “wallet funded” after the atomic wallet debit, but it must not say “restaurant settled” until the merchant payout is reconciled. Keep restaurant wallet payment disabled until a real merchant, valid wallet funding, payout controls, refunds, receipt email and operational reconciliation are verified. Never call the sample loan flow an approved live loan.
